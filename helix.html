The problem

A health and wellness company developed lifestyle plans based on a person’s genome, blood markers, stress hormones and gut microbiome. The program cost $3,000 with a one-year commitment.

The company was looking for strategies to nurture people into the flagship program.

The solutionA “light” version of the program with a limited set of genetic data and one coaching session, at a price point of $50.

Discovery & research

The main program required a blood draw scheduled at a clinical lab. This new product used saliva collected at home, a change that dramatically simplified the journey.

User journey

Kits are delivered, the customer sets up their online dashboard, sends the kit back, waits for results, gets an email when results are processed, and finally schedules a call with a coach.

User flow

Mapping the journey revealed a lot of unknown time between stages. We had no idea how long people would take to return kits, and only a rough estimate for DNA processing.

Dashboard states

The dashboard needed three key states: before genetic data, after data was processed, and after the coaching call. The most important was the empty state (keeping people engaged while they waited) and then making the dashboard come alive when data arrived.
